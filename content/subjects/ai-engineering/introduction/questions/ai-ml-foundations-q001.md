## Answer

AI is the broad engineering goal of building systems that perform tasks associated
with intelligence. Machine learning is a family of methods that learn a relationship
from data instead of specifying every behavior as a rule. Deep learning is machine
learning using multi-layer neural networks. Therefore deep learning is a subset of
machine learning, and machine learning is one approach used within AI. A rule engine
or search system can also be part of an AI product.

### What a strong answer adds

The terms describe different levels of abstraction. They do not guarantee accuracy,
autonomy, or understanding. A production AI feature may combine rules, retrieval,
classical ML, and a deep model.
