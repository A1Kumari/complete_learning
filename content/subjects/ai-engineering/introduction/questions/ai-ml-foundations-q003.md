## Answer

A discriminative task predicts a label, score, or decision for an input, such as
whether a document is a contract. A generative task produces new content or data,
such as a summary or a continuation of text. They are task descriptions, not a
guarantee about one model family. A product can use a classifier to route a request
and a language model to draft the response. Generation describes production of
likely outputs, not truth or authority.
